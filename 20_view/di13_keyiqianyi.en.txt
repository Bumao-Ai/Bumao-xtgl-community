13. Portability: Must Be Able to Be Taken Away (the whole project)
This chapter turns "still runs after changing the path, the drive letter, and the port" into machine criteria, executed by B27 during check --strict; a static hit is FAIL, and dynamic live testing is separately recorded to the delivery list by portability_probe.py.
13.0 Mandatory-07 The whole project must be portable:
(1) Criterion: within the release artifacts and the three-end active areas there must be no hard-coded drive-letter absolute path, user-directory absolute path, or interpreter absolute path (*python.exe); the port, project root, and key location must be obtained from in-package configuration files or environment variables, and must not depend on the local machine's registry, scheduled tasks, or desktop shortcuts. Internal tools and the 90_lishi archive area may retain local paths as needed, but they must not enter the release artifacts.
(2) Trigger point: every check --strict scans the scripts, documents, and batch files of 10_rac and 20_view\web; before release, copy the whole tree to another drive letter / level, change the 88xx port, start the three ends from scratch, and run check --strict.
(3) Handling: B27 judges FAIL and prints the file, line number, and hit fragment; when dynamic live testing does not pass, fill the "portability live test" column of the delivery list with "not passed" and attach a conclusion line, and the whole batch must not be released.
(4) Gate position: B27; the dynamic live-test tool is kernel\bumao_core\portability_probe.py.
